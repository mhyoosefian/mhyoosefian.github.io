<p class="intro">Hello! I’m Mohammad Hussein Yoosefian Nooshabadi!</p>

<div class="bio">
<p>I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at Northeastern University in Boston, advised by Prof. <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=5PBPqeQAAAAJ&amp;hl=en">Laurent Lessard</a>.</p>

<p>I am broadly interested in estimation and control theory, convex optimization, and computer vision. My current research focuses on state estimation for dynamical systems under non-Gaussian noise. I develop robust estimators that are computationally efficient and come with provable stability guarantees. My previous research topics include visual-inertial navigation and analytical dynamics. Prior to joining Northeastern, I received my M.Sc. from Sharif University of Technology and my B.Sc. from Isfahan University of Technology.</p>

<p>Beyond research, I enjoy traveling, reading Persian poetry and listening to quality music!</p>
</div>
